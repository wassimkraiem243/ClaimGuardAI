1. Company Introduction &
Challenge Context
Velodoc (by Amazit – FZCO) operates in the
HealthTech & AI Solutions sector, specializing
in AI-driven healthcare operational
automation, claims workflow simplification,
and administrative intelligence. To drive
digital transformation and address
emerging challenges, the company
continuously explores innovations in Agentic
AI & LLM Systems, Healthcare Interoperability
(HL7 FHIR R4), and Cybersecurity, Privacy &
Auditability.
In today
'
s rapidly evolving landscape, where

human-in-the-loop AI automation and real-
time administrative validation is increasingly

critical, we currently rely on manual claim
review processes and rigid, traditional rule
scripts. However, as our operations scale, we
are actively seeking innovative approaches
to automate pre-validation of healthcare
claim packages, drastically reduce
rejections and processing delays, and
maintain total transparency, privacy, and
auditability.

2. Problem Statement

To design, prototype, and demonstrate a
human-supervised Agentic AI Copilot
system that ingests synthetic healthcare
claim packages, detects administrative risks
and policy inconsistencies, generates
human-understandable explanations and
recommendations, and logs all actions for
auditability.
Our main challenge is that healthcare
claims are frequently delayed or rejected
because administrative information is
incomplete, inconsistent, duplicated, or
unsupported at the point of submission.
When high-volume synthetic claim
packages with complex coverage rules,
missing attachments, or data anomalies are
submitted, our systems experience
administrative bottlenecks, operational
delays, and high denial rates. We require a
robust solution capable of automatically
and securely handling claim pre-validation,
rule-linked evidence explanation, corrective
action recommendations, and human
escalation routing without disrupting
existing administrative infrastructure or
making unauthorized clinical decisions.
3. Technical Mission

Participating teams must build a system
evaluated across the following core phases:
Challenge Objective:

Total score: 50 points
Required Features:

Data Ingestion & Normalization
[15 Points]: Ingest synthetic claim
packages (FHIR R4 JSON / CSV)
containing patient, encounter, coverage,
provider, diagnosis, and claim line items,
and normalize them into a consistent
internal representation.
Deterministic & AI Rule Engine
[15 Points]: Implement an automated
validation engine executing against the
fictional payer-rule catalogue (10-15
rules) to detect missing, inconsistent,
duplicate, or unsupported data.
Explainability & Structured Output
[10 Points]: Generate structured,
machine-readable validation findings
containing Claim ID, Rule ID, rule-linked
evidence, severity level, confidence
score, and suggested corrective action.
Audit Log Engine [10 Points]:
Systematically record an auditable,
immutable log of all checks, AI
recommendations, confidence scores,
and system decisions.
Required Deliverables :
Source Architecture Diagram & Data
Flow Documentation.
A short video demonstration of the MVP.
Code repository with clear installation
and execution instructions.

Phase 2: Integration & Testing
Total score: 30 Points
Required Features:
Detection Quality & Benchmark
Performance[15 Points]: Evaluate
system performance on the validation
dataset (50 claims) achieving high
Macro F1 score across rule categories
and preserving valid claims (low false-
positive rate).
Human-in-the-Loop & Escalation Logic
[10 Points]: Implement reviewer-facing
routing that flags low-confidence or
high-severity cases for explicit human
approval, allowing manual overrides
and feedback logging.
Privacy, Security & Safety Guards
[5 Points]: Implement data
minimization, access control, prompt
/data security guards (preventing
hallucinated clinical advice), and
graceful error handling for malformed
FHIR inputs.
Required Deliverables :
Test evaluation report (detection
metrics, F1 score, false positive rate,
latency, and limitations).
Privacy and Security note (threat model,
access control, least privilege, and
auditability).
